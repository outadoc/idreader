An Android application that lets you read EU ID cards via NFC directly from your smartphone.

Built with privacy and security as core principles, this app gives you access to your electronic identity document without compromising your personal data:

- No Internet access, no data is extracted or sent to any server
- No document data is stored on your device after reading
- Your document password (CAN) is stored encrypted using Android Keystore with strong encryption keys

The app uses standard, public communication protocols defined in ICAO Doc 9303 and BSI TR-03110 to securely communicate with your ID card.

Currently tested and working with French CNIe cards. More documents and devices may be compatible.
